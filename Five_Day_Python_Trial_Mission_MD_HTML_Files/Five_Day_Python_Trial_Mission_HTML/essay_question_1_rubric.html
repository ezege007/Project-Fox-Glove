AI Grading Rubric — Easy Question 1

Goal: Reward basic understanding, not advanced wording. Ignore minor grammar or spelling mistakes if the meaning is clear.

  • Full credit: The learner clearly says AI can be wrong, incomplete, misleading, or not match the requirement and gives one reasonable verification example, such as running tests, checking the requirement, checking a boundary case, or confirming a factual claim with a reliable source.
  • Partial credit: The learner says AI should be checked but gives no clear reason, or gives a reasonable checking example without clearly explaining why checking is needed.
  • No credit: The response says AI should always be trusted, gives an unrelated answer, or provides no meaningful response.

Do not require: technical AI terminology, the word “hallucination,” or a long answer.
